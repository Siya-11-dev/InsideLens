AI Hackathon 
Overview
Microsoft and the Premier League invite developers worldwide to join Inside the Game: Developer Hackathon and build AI-powered solutions that transform synthetic, football-realistic data into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, and automated match recaps for studio, broadcast, or streaming scenarios.

Key dates

Registration deadline: October 20, 2026, 12:00 PM Pacific Time
Hacking period: October 6–October 27, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific Time
 

Create and submit an AI solution to climb the leaderboard and compete for a share of $50,000 USD in cash prizes, event tickets, and more.

The strongest submissions will leverage GitHub, GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Foundry, Azure AI Services, Microsoft Fabric and other Azure Database and Analytics services, and the Azure cloud-native services to create AI-powered applications and agents that:

Analyze match events in real time
Generate stories, highlights, and recaps
Explain key moments and tactical patterns
Expand datasets for AI and analytics
Deliver insights for fans around the world in multiple languages
Deliver personalized experiences for studios, streaming platforms, and fans
 

Get Started:

Register by October 20: Complete your registration by October 20, 2026, at 12:00 PM Pacific Time.
Watch for your invitation from Microsoft, inviting you to join Innovation Studio Platform, the official hackathon platform.
Review Premier League Hackathon rules and regulations for all the details you need to participate, including the prize categories, judging criteria and submission process.
 

Submit Your Project:

Submit your project by 11:59 PM Pacific Time on October 27, 2026.  
The top six projects will be selected and notified by November 10, 2026.


More Updates:
Microsoft and the Premier League invite developers worldwide to go beyond the scoreline and reimagine how football can be understood, experienced, and shared.
Build an AI-powered solution that transforms synthetic, football-realistic data into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, automated recaps, and personalized experiences for fans, studios, broadcasters, and streaming platforms.
Football is more than a final score. Every pass, shot, tackle, change in possession, and shift in pressure can reveal a deeper story about the match.
Inside the Game: Developer Hackathon challenges you to build an AI-driven application or agent that turns synthetic football events into useful, understandable, and engaging match intelligence.
Important data requirement
Projects must use synthetic, football-realistic data. No real Premier League match data is redistributed to participants.
